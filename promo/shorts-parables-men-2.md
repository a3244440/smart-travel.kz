# Шортсы-притчи с мужчинами — часть 2

Формат как у готовых роликов: **3 клипа по 10 с** (выбор → наказание → награда), без слов, под нашид.
Склейка — на 9,4 и 19,4 с, итого 29 с.

**Как генерировать**
1. Сначала в ChatGPT — картинка-якорь героев (промпт «Герои»). Остальные картинки — в том же чате, добавляя: *«keep exactly the same faces and clothes as in the reference»*.
2. Каждая картинка = первый кадр клипа. В генератор видео: картинка + промпт «Видео».
3. В кадре только мужчины. Если генератор отказывает из-за детей — пишите «adult sons».

Общая приставка ко всем промптам:
> Photorealistic, cinematic, vertical 9:16, natural skin texture, shallow depth of field, no text, no watermark.

## 5. «Два сада» (притча из суры Аль-Кахф, 18:32–44)

**Мораль:** всё, что имеешь, — от Аллаха. Скажи «МашаАллах», а не «это моё».

**Герои**
```
Two men in their 30s standing at a wooden fence at golden hour. Man A (left): humble farmer, short beard, simple beige shirt with rolled sleeves, white skullcap, calm grateful smile, holding a small basket with a few dates. Man B (right): rich landowner, trimmed beard, expensive embroidered dark-green robe over a white thobe, gold rings, proud arrogant pose, arms spread wide. Behind Man B: a huge lush garden with grapevines, date palms and a river. Behind Man A: a small modest field with a few palm trees.
```

**Клип 1 — хвастовство**
- Картинка: `Same two men, same clothes. Man B gestures proudly at his enormous green garden full of grapes and palms, laughing; Man A looks at him calmly with a hand on his heart.`
- Видео: `Static camera. The rich man walks through his lush garden, touches heavy bunches of grapes, spreads his arms and laughs arrogantly, pointing at the modest field of the humble man. The humble man smiles gently, places his hand on his heart and looks up at the sky.`

**Клип 2 — сад погибает**
- Картинка: `The rich man in the dark-green robe standing in the middle of his garden, a dark storm cloud gathering above, wind starting to blow.`
- Видео: `A sudden storm sweeps over the garden. Grapevines wither and turn brown, palm trees collapse, fruit falls and rots, the river dries into cracked earth. The rich man turns around in panic, falls to his knees, wringing his hands over the ruined garden, dust swirling around him.`

**Клип 3 — скромное поле**
- Картинка: `The humble man in the beige shirt and white skullcap standing at the edge of his small field at sunrise, holding the basket.`
- Видео: `Soft golden sunrise. The humble man raises his hands in dua; his small field gently sways, palm trees grow taller and fill with ripe dates, green sprouts rise from the earth. He picks a date, smiles with gratitude, and kneels in sujood on the grass. Slow push-in.`

## 6. «Две лодки» (таваккуль)

**Мораль:** спасает не богатство, а упование на Аллаха.

**Герои**
```
Calm sea at a small harbor at dawn. Man A (left): fisherman, short beard, simple white shirt and grey trousers, skullcap, next to a small old wooden boat. Man B (right): rich man in white designer clothes, sunglasses, gold watch, next to a huge shiny white-and-gold luxury yacht.
```

**Клип 1 — отплытие**
- Картинка: `Same two men at the harbor. Man B steps proudly onto his luxury yacht, waving; Man A pushes his small wooden boat into the water.`
- Видео: `The rich man laughs and sails away on his glamorous yacht at high speed, filming himself on his phone. The fisherman calmly rows his small wooden boat out to sea, whispering a prayer.`

**Клип 2 — шторм**
- Картинка: `The luxury yacht in the middle of a dark stormy sea, huge waves, the rich man holding on to the railing.`
- Видео: `A violent storm. Giant waves crash over the yacht, its gold decorations break off, the engine smokes and dies, the yacht tilts and slowly sinks. The rich man clings to a floating piece of white plastic, soaked and terrified.`

**Клип 3 — спасение**
- Картинка: `The fisherman in his small wooden boat on the same stormy sea, raising his hands in dua, a beam of light breaking through the clouds above him.`
- Видео: `The fisherman makes dua; the clouds open, a warm beam of light falls on his boat and the sea around him becomes calm. He sees the rich man in the water, rows to him, pulls him into the boat. Both sit together as the sun rises over a peaceful sea.`

## 7. «Два мешка» (садака)

**Мораль:** садака не уменьшает имущество.

**Герои**
```
A desert caravan road at sunset, sand dunes. Man A (left): traveler with a short beard, light-brown robe, white turban, carrying a small cloth sack of bread. Man B (right): rich merchant in a rich red-and-gold robe, gold rings, carrying a large heavy sack of gold coins.
```

**Клип 1 — нищий старик**
- Картинка: `Same two men on the desert road. An exhausted old man with a white beard in torn clothes sits on the sand, stretching out his hand to them.`
- Видео: `The rich merchant turns away, clutches his gold sack tighter and walks past the old man. The traveler kneels, opens his small sack and gives the old man his only loaf of bread, smiling warmly. The old man raises his hands in dua for him.`

**Клип 2 — золото тянет вниз**
- Картинка: `The rich merchant alone in the dunes, bent under the huge sack of gold on his back.`
- Видео: `The gold sack grows heavier and heavier; the merchant's feet sink into the sand, he struggles and falls to his knees, the sack tears and gold coins pour into the sand and disappear like water. He digs desperately with his hands, finding only sand.`

**Клип 3 — мешок полон**
- Картинка: `The traveler in the light-brown robe sitting by a small campfire in the desert at night under a starry sky, his small cloth sack beside him.`
- Видео: `The traveler opens his small sack: it is full of warm fresh bread and dates, glowing softly. He breaks bread and shares it with other tired travelers who sit around his fire. Everyone smiles; the stars shine brightly above. Slow push-in.`

## 8. «Две лестницы» (слава в телефоне vs вечное)

**Мораль:** лайки исчезают, добрые дела остаются.

**Герои**
```
A modern city square at dusk. Man A (left): young man with a neat beard, white shirt and dark trousers, calm face, holding a prayer rug rolled under his arm. Man B (right): young influencer with trendy hair, designer hoodie, gold chains, holding a phone on a selfie stick. In front of them: a glowing golden escalator made of floating like-icons going up into the sky, and an old stone staircase leading up a hill to a small mosque.
```

**Клип 1 — выбор пути**
- Картинка: `Same two men at the foot of the two staircases. Man B steps onto the glowing golden escalator of like-icons, filming himself; Man A puts his foot on the first old stone step.`
- Видео: `The influencer rides the glowing escalator upward, hearts and like-icons explode around him, he poses and laughs for his phone. The young man in white slowly climbs the stone steps, step by step, calm.`

**Клип 2 — лайки гаснут**
- Картинка: `The influencer high in the sky on the glowing escalator, the screen of his phone flickering.`
- Видео: `The like-icons start to fade and pop like bubbles, the golden escalator cracks and dissolves into pixels beneath him. He falls back down to the empty grey square, his phone screen shatters. He sits alone on the ground staring at the broken black screen.`

**Клип 3 — вершина**
- Картинка: `The young man in white reaching the top of the stone staircase in front of a small beautiful mosque at sunrise.`
- Видео: `He reaches the top, unrolls his prayer rug in front of the mosque and prays as the sun rises over the city below. After the prayer he turns, sees the fallen influencer at the bottom, walks back down and offers him his hand. Both walk up the steps together.`

## 9. «Песочные часы» (время)

**Мораль:** время — это жизнь. На что ты его тратишь?

**Герои**
```
A cozy old library-café with wooden shelves at golden hour. Two men sit at one table. Man A (left): calm young man with a short beard, white shirt, a Quran on a wooden stand in front of him. Man B (right): young man in a trendy jacket, earbuds, scrolling a phone with a bored face, a game controller next to him. On the table in front of each man stands an hourglass: a golden one in front of Man B, a simple wooden one in front of Man A.
```

**Клип 1 — песок течёт**
- Картинка: `Same two men at the table, both hourglasses just turned over, sand starting to fall.`
- Видео: `The young man in the jacket scrolls endlessly, laughing at videos; the sand in his golden hourglass pours down fast. The calm man reads the Quran and then gets up to help an old man carry books; the sand in his wooden hourglass falls and each grain turns into a small floating light.`

**Клип 2 — пусто**
- Картинка: `The young man in the jacket looking up from his phone, noticing his golden hourglass is almost empty.`
- Видео: `The last sand runs out of the golden hourglass. Everything around him turns grey; the phone shows a black screen; he grabs the hourglass and shakes it, but it is empty. He looks at his hands with regret as the light in the room fades.`

**Клип 3 — свет**
- Картинка: `The calm man in the white shirt holding his wooden hourglass, surrounded by soft floating lights.`
- Видео: `The floating lights from his hourglass gather around him and form a glowing path leading out of the library toward a sunlit mosque. He closes the Quran, smiles, and walks along the path of light. Slow camera follow.`

## 10. «Два подарка отцу» (почитание родителей)

**Мораль:** отцу нужен ты, а не подарок.

**Герои**
```
An old Kazakh courtyard house with a wooden porch at sunset, an elderly father with a white beard, chapan and tubeteika sitting alone on a bench. Two adult sons in their 30s: Son A — modest, short beard, simple shirt and vest, carrying a thermos of tea and a bag of bread; Son B — businessman in an expensive suit, on a phone call, with a huge gift box wrapped in gold.
```

**Клип 1 — визит**
- Картинка: `Same three men in the courtyard. Son B hands the huge gold-wrapped box to the father without looking at him, still talking on the phone; Son A stands behind with the thermos.`
- Видео: `The businessman drops the huge golden gift box next to his father, glances at his watch, keeps talking on the phone and quickly leaves to his black car. The father looks at the box, his smile fading.`

**Клип 2 — коробка пуста**
- Картинка: `The elderly father opening the huge golden gift box alone on the bench.`
- Видео: `He opens the box: inside is an expensive gadget that he doesn't understand; he puts it aside and sits lonely as the sun goes down. Meanwhile, in the city, the businessman sits in a glass office, rich but alone, the phone silent; he looks at an old photo of his father with regret.`

**Клип 3 — чай с отцом**
- Картинка: `Son A sitting next to his elderly father on the porch, pouring tea from a thermos into a small bowl (kese).`
- Видео: `The modest son kisses his father's hand, pours him tea, breaks bread and listens to his stories. The father laughs, then raises his hands and makes dua for his son; a soft golden light surrounds them. The son hugs his father. Warm sunset, slow push-in.`
